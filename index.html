Method

Runs stop on first entry into the recurrent set. Exact probabilities and mean exit times solve (I − Q)B = S and (I − Q)t = 1. The 95% interval is a normal approximation for the empirical mean.

Every run is shown as a point. Filled strips below each state show its share of runs. Playback shows at most 200 steps; estimates use complete trajectories up to the configured step limit. Overlapping points are counted in the state totals.
