A NOTE IN THE MARGIN

Arriving isn’t always
the same as staying.

An absorbing state keeps you forever. A recurrent class can keep you moving between its members. Here, each trajectory stops at the first recurrent or absorbing state it enters; that is the outcome we count.

For transient-to-transient probabilities Q and transient-to-recurrent probabilities S, the exact answers solve (I − Q)B = S and (I − Q)t = 1. The simulation estimates the same quantities, one random walk at a time.

The interval describes Monte Carlo uncertainty in the mean. A correct simulation can miss a 95% interval about one time in twenty; it is not a pass/fail test.
